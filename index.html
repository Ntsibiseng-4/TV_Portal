THE PROBLEM

Education Inequality Is a System Crisis

Millions of children across South Africa and developing regions are beings left behind by a system that requires infrastructure they simply don't have.

10.4%

Limited Internet Access

Reliable household internet access Most digital learning platforms require stable internet, yet only a small percentage of South African households have reliable internet access, leaving many learners disconnected from online education.

23.4%

Limited Access to Learning Devices

Households lack access to computers or tablets Many students cannot participate in digital learning because they do not own smartphones, tablets, or computers required by most educational platforms.

3M+

Fragmented Learning Resources

No single learning hub Educational content is spread across many websites and applications, making it difficult for learners to find curriculum-aligned resources in one secure place.

4 388

Growing Cybersecurity Risks

Weekly cyberattacks on education Educational institutions face thousands of cyberattacks every week, highlighting the need for a secure and trusted digital learning environment.

The Solution Isn't Building More Devices—It's Unlocking the Potential of the One Families Already Have.
Nearly 79% of South African households already own a television, making it one of the most widely available technologies in the country. EduSmart TV transforms this everyday device into an intelligent learning portal, giving learners access to curriculum-aligned education, AI-powered support, and secure digital learning- nointernet connection required and no new devices needed.

PLATFORM FEATURES

Everything a Learner Needs, Delivered via TV

Six powerful pillars designed from the ground up for learners in underserved communities - built for their realities.

CORE AI
AI-Powered Personalized Learning

EduSmart TV uses Artificial Intelligence to understand each learner's progress, strengths, and learning pace. It recommends personalised lessons, identifies knowledge gaps, and adapts the learning experience to help every student succeed.

ALWAYS AVAILABLE
Internet-Independent Learning

All content educational content are delivered through satellite-enabled television infrastructure, eliminating the need for reliable home internet or expensive mobile data. Learners can access quality education using the television they already have.

TV NATIVE
Interactive TV Learning

Transform any compatible television into an engaging digital device where learners can watch lessons, complete activities, explore educational content, and navigate using a simple TV remote.

SAFE
Digital Safety & Cybersecurity

Built-in child safey controls, content filtering, and age-appropriate guardians ensure a secure nd trusted learning environment.

LIBRARY
Educational Resource Library

Access a centralized collection of curriculum-aligned textbooks, video lessons, revision materials, interactive e-books, and supplementary reading or educational resources—all from one platform.

GAMIFIED
Gamified Learning Tools

Quizzes, challenges, badges, and leaderboards making learning addictive and rewarding. Learners earn achievements as they progress.
